SmartCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

SmartCalc ("the app") is a notepad calculator for Android, published by Parvez Iqbal Issa.

Data we collect

None. SmartCalc does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app does not request internet access, and it has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your notes and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
